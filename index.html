OpenID Connect provider, served as static files

Sign in to apps with your Trezor

There is no server here. Apps send you to this site, your Trezor signs an ID token over USB, and the browser hands it back to the app. Apps check the signature against the public keys published on this site.

Every sign-in is confirmed on the device. The signing key never leaves it and is derived from your wallet seed with SLIP-0013.

Works in Chrome, Edge and Brave (WebUSB) with Trezor Model One (firmware 1.12.1 or newer), Model T, Safe 3 and Safe 5. Close Trezor Suite first; only one app can use the device at a time.

What apps need to know

This provider only supports the implicit flow: response_type=id_token with a nonce, returned in the URL fragment or by form_post. There is no token endpoint and no access token. Tokens are ES256 and short-lived; the subject is the username registered in setup.
